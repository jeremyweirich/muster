## Story

Generations ago, the Wardens, Goblins and Elementals fought a war that nearly wrecked the world. It ended when the three factions were finally able to find Common Ground. Now every dispute over land, insults and old grudges is settled on the Field. Each commander chooses and commits their units carefully - once battle starts, they cannot be moved again.

The campaigns:

- **The Drill Book:** A nervous new Marshal learns the ropes.
- **The Tinker's Notebook:** "Sometimes you gotta fight dirty to win." *(not built yet)*
- **The Volatile Grimoire:** "We're not being rude. We're standing over here so you don't catch fire." *(not built yet)*

## How to play

Shown the first time the site opens on Play, and from the header's How to play button:

### Build, then watch

Pick units and place them in your **blue zone**. Press **Fight!** and the battle plays out on its own.

### Survivors hit HP

Squads still standing after a fight damage the other side's HP. First to **0** loses. Your army stays, and each round you add more.

### Counter what you see

You can see the enemy's army from last round. Hover any unit to see what it beats.

You're playing the AI on Easy. Change it at the bottom of the sidebar.

After the Wardens campaign's finale, a version about what the campaign didn't cover:

### Every unit, every faction

The campaign was Wardens against goblins. Here the shop has every unit: Wardens, Goblins, **Elementals**, and three independents for hire. The AI can field any of them too.

### Round 1: pick a pack

Units start locked. In round 1, take one of three **packs** (two units unlocked, with squads already placed) or the **war chest** (700 supply, nothing unlocked). Later, unlocking a unit costs 100, or 200 for an independent.

### Elements combine

Elementals leave statuses behind, like Wet or Burning, and a second element on top sets off a reaction: lightning hits a Wet unit twice as hard, and frost freezes it solid. Statuses hit whoever is there, your own units too.

The AI starts on Easy. Normal and Hard are at the bottom of the sidebar.

## Units

The Units tab and the shop: each unit's roles and one-line description, its four upgrades, and the caption under its showcase fight.

### Wardens

#### Knights

*Sturdy, Melee*

An armored wall that cuts down anything that walks into it. Heavy crossbow bolts and fliers are its problem.

- **Shield Wall:** +25% HP and +20% physical resistance.
- **Lance Charge:** 30% faster, and the first strike deals ×4 damage.
- **Warded Plate:** +50% resistance to fire, frost, lightning and water.
- **Veterans:** +30% damage, and each knight blocks the first hit it takes.

Showcase (2× Knights against 3× Grunts): A wall the goblin mob runs onto.

#### Crossbowmen

*Ranged, Anti-air*

A few heavy crossbows. Every bolt hits like a hammer and punches through armor, but a swarm soaks them up, and they're slow to aim at fliers (half damage) until they learn Skyhunters.

- **Longshot:** +3 range.
- **Fire Bolts:** Fire damage, +25%, that ignites Oiled targets.
- **Repeaters:** Shoot 2.5× as fast for 60% damage per bolt.
- **Skyhunters:** Full damage to fliers (not half), picking them out first, with +2 range against them.

Showcase (2× Crossbowmen against Knights): Heavy bolts punch through the Knights' armor before they arrive.

#### Ballista

*Sniper, Anti-large, Anti-air, Slow*

Huge single bolts at very long range. It picks the biggest target nearby and winds up every shot. Swarms and raiders beat it.

- **Heavy Bolts:** +50% damage.
- **Repeater:** Fires twice as fast for 60% damage per bolt.
- **Chain Harpoons:** Bolts Chill. A second hit freezes; the next shatters for double.
- **Reinforced Frame:** +100% HP and +30% physical resistance.

Showcase (2× Ballista against Ogre Brute): Double damage against big targets, from across the field.

#### Griffon Riders

*Flier, Fast, Skirmisher*

Halflings on griffons, hurling spears from just above the fight: ×1.5 damage to medium units, fliers among them. Fast, and they ignore the ground and its hazards.

- **Dive Attack:** The first strike deals ×4 damage.
- **Armored Saddles:** +40% HP and +20% physical resistance.
- **Sky Patrol:** Pick out nearby fliers first, with ×1.75 damage to them.
- **Giant Slayers:** ×2.5 damage to large and huge units, and pick them out when near.

Showcase (Griffon Riders against Knights): Knights can't hit fliers, so the spears rain down unanswered.

#### Cleric

*Healer, Support*

Priests who don't fight. Every 5s they heal the units around them by 250. A unit they heal can't be healed by another Cleric's prayer for 5s, so more Clerics heal more units, not the same ones faster.

- **Constant Prayer:** Heal 70 every second instead of 250 every 5s: more healing, and less of it wasted.
- **Blessing:** Allies nearby deal +30% damage.
- **Purify:** Every 1.5s, clears all statuses from nearby allies.
- **Battle Hymn:** Allies nearby attack 30% faster and move 25% faster.

Showcase (Cleric with Knights against Knights): Two equal lines of Knights. The one with a Cleric behind it wins.

#### Dwarven Pikemen

*Anti-large, Swarm, Melee*

A long line of dwarven pikes. Plenty of sturdy bodies soak up big blows, and the long pikes deal ×4.5 damage to large units and cavalry. Fire and splash cut through the ranks.

- **Pike Wall:** Melee attackers take 40 damage back on every hit.
- **Long Pikes:** +1 range, so the back rank fights too.
- **Heavy Halberds:** +40% damage, and every blow sweeps a wide arc.
- **Drilled Ranks:** +3 dwarves per squad.

Showcase (3× Dwarven Pikemen against 2× Ogre Brute): Pikes deal ×3.5 against large units. The Ogres walk onto them.

### Goblins

#### Grunts

*Swarm, Fast, Melee, Glass cannon*

A screaming mob. Most blows kill a goblin, but there are always more of them, and a mob left alone stabs anything to pieces.

- **Scrap Armor:** +20 HP, and each goblin blocks the first hit it takes. 15% slower.
- **Feral Frenzy:** Run 30% faster and stab 40% faster.
- **Party Time:** +8 goblins per squad.
- **Oil Bombs:** Goblins spill oil where they die. Oil slows; fire makes it burning ground.

Showcase (2× Grunts against 2× Crossbowmen): Too many goblins for a few bolts: the swarm soaks them and reaches the shooters.

#### Skirmishers

*Skirmisher, Anti-air, Swarm*

Goblin archers with short bows. Cheap and fragile, they shoot from just behind the mob, and they can hit fliers.

- **Ambush:** Can't be targeted except for 2s after each shot. Splash still hits.
- **Barbed Arrows:** +60% damage.
- **Improvised Bombs:** One fire grenade per squad: a wide blast that sets all in it Burning.
- **Recruiters:** Every kill pulls another goblin archer into the squad for the rest of the battle.

Showcase (2× Skirmishers against Giant Bats): Short bows that hit fliers: the bats dive into a cloud of arrows.

#### Wolf Riders

*Fast, Raider, Skirmisher*

Goblins on big wolves: the fastest thing on the ground, and tough enough to crash through to the back line. The first javelin hits hard.

- **Dire Wolves:** +50% HP and +20% damage.
- **Tower Raiders:** 15% faster, and pick out a Keep over the army when one is near.
- **Flankers:** Pick out nearby archers, casters and siege, with ×1.5 damage to them.
- **Bloodthirst:** Every kill readies another ×5 charge javelin.

Showcase (Wolf Riders against 2× Crossbowmen): The fastest unit on the Field reaches the shooters before they can thin it.

#### Troll

*Anti-large, Sturdy, Regenerates, Melee*

Two tough melee brutes. They attack faster while at full HP, and each hit in a row on the same target deals more damage. They regenerate HP, except while Burning.

- **Thick Blood:** Regenerates an additional 20 HP/s, even while Burning.
- **Gorge:** Each hit heals it.
- **Rock Skin:** +40% HP.
- **Uprooted Tree:** Swings a whole tree: +1 range, out of reach of pikes hitting back, and each hit splashes a little, for 20% less damage.

Showcase (2× Troll against Stone Golem): Each hit on the same target hits harder. The rage builds on one big Golem.

#### Ogre Brute

*Sturdy, Splash, Slow, Melee*

A huge, slow wall of meat with a cleaving club. Takes extra fire damage.

- **Thick Skull:** +40% HP, no fire weakness. 40% of hits kill a nearby goblin instead.
- **Rampage:** Swings 50% faster and moves 30% faster.
- **Wrecking Swing:** +0.8 splash radius and +20% damage.
- **Warboss:** Nearby goblins hit 25% harder and faster. Calls a grunt every 5s.

Showcase (Ogre Brute against 3× Grunts): A cleaving club: every swing splashes through the mob.

#### Giant Bats

*Flier, Fast, Raider*

Goblins on giant bats. A battle opens with a dive: they fly 16 tiles straight ahead without attacking, taking 25% less physical damage. Where they land, they pick out archers, casters and siege crews. Their claws reach fliers.

- **Long Dive:** The dive goes 40% further (22 tiles), deep enough to reach the back of the enemy's deployment.
- **Shriek:** At their first target, then every 10s: enemies around it are Dazed for 3s.
- **Bloodsuckers:** No dive: they pick targets from the start, fliers first, and heal for 40% of the damage they deal.
- **Savage Claws:** +15% HP and +60% damage.

Showcase (Giant Bats against 2× Ballista): They dive straight over the Field, shrugging off bolts, and land on the Ballistae.

### Elementals

#### Fire Elemental

*Splash, Ranged, Slow*

A knot of four living flames that lob slow fireballs, setting whole groups alight.

- **Controlled Burn:** +20% damage, and its flames only catch on enemies.
- **Meteor:** +4 range, +50% damage and a wider blast, but fires 30% slower.
- **Wildfire:** Fireballs leave burning ground, which burns allies too.
- **Living Inferno:** Burns enemies within 1.5 tiles (10/s). Melee attackers catch fire.

Showcase (Fire Elemental against 2× Grunts): Fireballs splash a crowd and leave it burning.

#### Water Elemental

*Sturdy, Combo*

A big, tough wave that soaks everything it hits. Wet enemies conduct lightning and freeze easily.

- **Glacial Heart:** +40% HP. Attackers are Chilled; hit it twice and they freeze.
- **Tidal Surge:** +60% damage, +3 range, and hits leave puddles.
- **Wellspring:** Heals nearby allies 40 every 2s.
- **Tsunami:** +1 splash radius and +25% damage.

Showcase (Water Elemental with Air Elemental against Knights, Grunts): A tidal tank that soaks the enemy Wet, so its partner's lightning conducts for double damage.

#### Air Elemental

*Ranged, Anti-air, Combo*

A pair of whirlwinds that throw chain lightning at close range. Wet targets take double. They can learn to fly.

- **Thunderhead:** Lightning jumps 3 more times and hits 20% harder.
- **Tempest:** +40% HP and attacks 40% faster.
- **Eye of the Storm:** ×2 damage to fliers (×3 in all), and picks them out first.
- **Take Flight:** Becomes a flier, 40% faster, floating over ground hazards.

Showcase (Air Elemental against 2× Skirmishers): Chain lightning jumps from goblin to goblin.

#### Cloudlings

*Swarm, Flier, Fast*

A flock of little rainclouds that pelt the ground with hail. Fast and fragile, and only anti-air can reach them.

- **Downpour:** Hails 20% faster. Rains on its first target, then every 6s: soaks it Wet, leaves a puddle.
- **Thunderstorm:** Calls lightning on its first target, then every 10s. ×2 vs Wet; shatters Frozen.
- **Overcast:** +6 clouds per squad.
- **Freezing Rain:** +40% damage, as frost that ignores armor and freezes Wet targets.

Showcase (3× Cloudlings against 2× Crossbowmen): A flock the bolts can't thin: each heavy bolt only ever pops one cloud.

#### Frost Elemental

*Anti-large, Ranged, Anti-air*

Three ice spirits with long-range frost bolts. The cold sinks in: each bolt in a row on one target hits harder, so they bring down giants, but swarms waste them. Melts fast to fire.

- **Chilling Bolts:** Bolts Chill. The next frost hit on a Chilled target freezes it.
- **Cold Snap:** Shoots 50% faster.
- **Hoarfrost:** Every 2s, enemies within 2.5 tiles are Chilled.
- **Glacial Reach:** +4 range, −25% damage. Picks out archers, casters and siege first.

Showcase (2× Frost Elemental against Stone Golem): The cold sinks in: each bolt in a row on the Golem hits harder than the last.

#### Stone Golem

*Sturdy, Melee, Slow*

A towering mass of living rock. Slow and heavy, it shrugs off lightning and fire, but water wears it away and pikes and swarms can bring it down.

- **Granite:** +40% HP and +20% physical resistance.
- **Quake:** Slams splash and stun everything around for 0.5s.
- **Magma Core:** Fire damage that sets enemies Burning.
- **Landslide:** 60% faster, and its first slam deals ×3.

Showcase (Stone Golem against Knights): Too big to cut down: the Knights' swords barely scratch it.

### Independents

#### Dragon

*Flier, Splash, Sturdy*

Breathes fire on everything below. Ballistae are its bane.

- **Inferno:** +25% damage, and its breath leaves burning ground.
- **Ancient Scales:** Toughness 15: every hit it takes is 15 lower, so arrows and claws barely scratch it.
- **Elder Blood:** Regenerates 40 HP/s, except while Chilled.
- **Dread:** Everything its breath hits is Dazed for 1.5s.

Showcase (Dragon against 3× Dwarven Pikemen): Fire splashes the ranks from the air, where pikes can't reach.

#### Giant

*Splash, Sturdy, Slow, Ranged*

Hurls boulders that crush whole formations. Very slow.

- **Earthshaker:** Boulders briefly stun everything they land on.
- **Thick Hide:** +40% HP and +25% physical resistance.
- **Rockslide:** +3 range and a wider blast.
- **Frost Giant:** Frost boulders that Chill, and freeze Wet or Chilled targets.

Showcase (Giant against 4× Grunts): Boulders land on the mob and flatten goblins by the handful.

#### Bog Toad Riders

*Skirmisher, Anti-air, Fast*

Swamp folk on giant toads, hiring out to whoever fights near their marsh. Long sticky tongues lash out, snatch fliers from the air and hit medium units hardest. Pikes and swarms are their problem.

- **Fat Toads:** +40% damage.
- **Spit Bile:** Spit instead of lick: every hit splashes a small area, for 40% less damage.
- **Gulp:** Every lick sticks: the target is Snared for 2s.
- **Long Tongues:** +2 range.

Showcase (Bog Toad Riders against Griffon Riders): Sticky tongues snatch fliers out of the air, and medium units taste best: the Griffons never land a spear.

## Codex

Everything a new Warden needs to know about the Field. Underlined words elsewhere in the game link to their entry here.

### The Compact

#### How a match works

Generations ago, the three peoples fought a war that nearly wrecked the world. It ended in a treaty, the Compact. Now every dispute is settled on the Field, under the same rules for everyone.

1. **Recruit.** Spend supply on squads from the sidebar.
2. **Place.** Put them in your blue zone, the back 7 rows of the Field. `R` rotates a squad, `Ctrl+Z` undoes a purchase.
3. **Hands off.** When the horn sounds, the battle plays itself, for up to 120 seconds. A side loses the battle when its whole army is gone. You never control units in a fight: the skill is in what you pick and where you put it.

#### Rounds and HP

- Your army stays for the whole match. Squads from earlier rounds are **locked** where you put them, and each round you add more. Every unit is fully restored after each battle.
- While you build, you see the enemy's army **as it stood last round**, plus their unlocks and upgrades. You never see what they're adding right now, and they never see yours. Guess their answer to your army, and answer that.
- Supply: round 1's comes from your opening, then 400 in round 2, 500 in round 3, and 100 more each round after. Anything you don't spend carries over.
- The loser of a battle loses HP: 4 for every enemy squad still standing, plus 8 for every 100 supply of them still alive. If time runs out, both sides lose HP for the other's survivors. Each side starts with 400 HP, and the first to reach 0 loses the match.

#### Ground and air

The Field has two layers. Most units walk on the ground. Fliers move above it: they pass over ground units and ignore everything on the ground.

Every attack hits the ground, the air, or both, and each unit's card says which. If the enemy flies and your army can only hit the ground, you can't win that fight.

Can hit air: Crossbowmen, Ballista, Griffon Riders, Skirmishers, Giant Bats, Air Elemental, Cloudlings, Frost Elemental, Dragon, Bog Toad Riders

#### Keeps

Each side has two Keeps near the back of its zone (150 HP, no weapons). Keeps don't count toward winning a battle, but when one falls, its owner's **whole army** is Dazed for 4s. A single raider that slips around the flank and topples a Keep can swing the main fight.

Guard your flanks, or buy insurance in the Upgrades tab: each tower level costs 100 / 200 / 400 / 800, adds 2,000 HP and shortens the Daze by 0.5s (to at least 2s).

#### Who gets hurt

Attacks, and the splash around them, only hurt enemies. Everything elemental is **neutral**: statuses, surfaces and reactions hit whoever is there, your own units included. Your fire can burn your own Knights, and the enemy's lightning can electrify a puddle you made. The battle report counts this as friendly fire.

#### Picking targets

Each unit picks the nearest enemy it can hit, unless its card says it prefers something else nearby, and walks to it. On the way it looks round about once a second and may switch. Once it starts attacking, it **keeps its target** until that enemy dies, hides or gets out of range, whatever else walks up to it. Raiders that arrive while the back line is busy with something else get a free moment.

#### Winding up

Most units fire the moment they've reloaded. A few heavy weapons, like the Ballista, **wind up** every shot: once reloaded, they spend a moment aiming at their target first (a line shows where), and only fire if it's still in range. Each new target means a fresh windup, so many small enemies waste a lot of their time.

#### Diving

Giant Bats open every battle with a **dive**: they fly a fixed distance straight ahead, over everything, without picking a target or attacking, and take less physical damage on the way. Only when the dive ends do they look for enemies. So where you place them decides where they land: put them so the dive ends on the enemy's back line, remembering that both armies move while the bats are in the air. Their card says how far they dive.

### Terrain

#### Maps

Every Field has its own ground. Terrain lies only in no-man's land, and each map is the same from both sides, turned round. Your units don't plan around it: they head straight for their target and walk round whatever is in the way. Fliers ignore the ground entirely. Placing your squads so the ground works for you, and not for them, is up to you.

Maps: Open Field, Millbrook Ford, Standing Stones, Long Grass, The Mire, Broken Hills, The Furrows, Border Pass. Some story missions have a map of their own.

#### Rocks

Ground units can't cross rocks: they walk round them. Fliers pass over.

#### Pond

Deep water. Ground units can't cross it: they walk round. Fliers pass over.

#### Rough ground

Ground units cross it at half speed. Fliers aren't slowed.

#### Bridge

The way across a river for ground units. Fliers cross anywhere.

#### Tall grass

Hides ground units from ground shooters. A unit in it can be shot only for 2 s after it attacks. Melee, fliers' attacks and splash still find it.

### Your army

#### Unlocks

Every unit has a one-time **unlock** price per match (🔒 in the recruit list), and then its normal price per squad. Your opening pack unlocks two for free. Switching to a perfect counter costs more than adding to what you already have. You can see which units the enemy has unlocked.

#### Upgrades

Every unit has four upgrades. An upgrade changes **every** squad of that unit, including the ones already on the Field. They cost 200 / 400 / 600 / 800 in the order you buy them, whatever the unit, so each one turns the unit into a more expensive specialist. The enemy's upgrades show beside their health bar.

#### Veterans

Squads earn XP for the damage they deal and the healing they do. At 2,000 / 9,000 / 27,400 / 70,300 total XP a squad earns level 2 / 3 / 4 / 5, and a gold ▲ appears over it. Each level takes many more good fights than the last. Click it and pay half the unit's price to promote it: a level-N squad has N× the HP and damage. A squad can only hold one unpaid level, so XP beyond it is lost until you promote.

#### The opening offer

Nothing is unlocked when a match starts. In round 1 you pick one of 3 **packs**, or the war chest. A pack is two units, both unlocked, with 400 supply's worth of their squads already placed for you (move them as you like, but they can't be sold), plus 200 supply to spend. The war chest is 700 supply and nothing unlocked: a little less once you pay for two unlocks, but you build exactly what you want. Both sides are offered the same packs, so you can guess what the enemy might start with.

### Roles

Roles say what a unit is for. Each unit has two to four.

#### Sturdy

Lots of HP or armor. Sturdy units stand in front so the damage dealers behind them live longer. Most hit softly for their cost, so they hold the line while something else wins the fight.

Units: Knights, Troll, Ogre Brute, Water Elemental, Stone Golem, Dragon, Giant

#### Swarm

Many cheap bodies in one squad. A huge single hit only kills one of them, so swarms soak up snipers and heavy hitters. Splash is their nightmare: one blast can clear a dozen.

Units: Dwarven Pikemen, Grunts, Skirmishers, Cloudlings

#### Fast

Gets there first. Fast units reach the enemy's back line and Keeps before the main fight starts. In your own army, a fast unit can run ahead of its friends and fight alone.

Units: Griffon Riders, Grunts, Wolf Riders, Giant Bats, Cloudlings, Bog Toad Riders

#### Slow

Arrives late. Slow units need a front line to stand behind, and fast raiders love to catch them alone.

Units: Ballista, Ogre Brute, Fire Elemental, Stone Golem, Giant

#### Sniper

Very long range, usually one target at a time. Snipers stay at the back and pick off the biggest targets. Anything that reaches them, or a swarm of small targets, beats them.

Units: Ballista

#### Ranged

Attacks from a distance, so it gets free hits while the enemy walks in. Keep ranged units behind a front line: if raiders or fliers reach them, they fold.

Units: Crossbowmen, Fire Elemental, Air Elemental, Frost Elemental, Giant

#### Melee

Has to get up close. Melee units take hits on the way in, and anything that punishes attackers (like a pike line) hurts them most. Once they arrive, they hit hard.

Units: Knights, Dwarven Pikemen, Grunts, Troll, Ogre Brute, Stone Golem

#### Skirmisher

Throws or shoots from just outside melee range, a step behind the front line. Skirmishers hit swords before swords can answer, but longer-ranged archers outshoot them.

Units: Griffon Riders, Skirmishers, Wolf Riders, Bog Toad Riders

#### Splash

Each hit also damages everything close to the target. Splash punishes swarms and tight formations. Single big units and spread-out squads take it best.

Units: Ogre Brute, Fire Elemental, Dragon, Giant

#### Anti-air

Can hit fliers. Most units can't, so every army needs some anti-air in case the enemy takes to the sky. A unit's card says what its attack can hit: ground, air, or both.

Units: Crossbowmen, Ballista, Skirmishers, Air Elemental, Frost Elemental, Bog Toad Riders

#### Anti-large

Bonus damage against big targets: ogres, trolls, golems and cavalry. Big units that charge into anti-large units regret it. Swarms of small units don't care.

Units: Ballista, Dwarven Pikemen, Troll, Frost Elemental

#### Flier

Flies over the field. Fliers pass over ground units without bumping into them and ignore everything on the ground: burning ground, oil, puddles and ice. Only attacks that hit air can reach them, so an army without anti-air can't fight back at all.

Units: Griffon Riders, Giant Bats, Cloudlings, Dragon

#### Support

Makes nearby allies better instead of fighting. Weak on its own. In battle it walks with your nearest ground squad that fights up close, or your nearest armed squad if there is none, and stays a couple of tiles behind it, out of the melee.

Units: Cleric

#### Healer

Restores allies' HP during the battle. Place healers just behind the line, within reach of the units taking the hits. Clerics' prayers don't stack: a unit healed by one can't be healed by another until that prayer comes round again, so more Clerics heal more units, not the same ones faster.

Units: Cleric

#### Combo

Sets up elemental reactions for other units, for example soaking targets so lightning hits twice as hard. A combo unit is modest alone and strong next to the right partner.

Units: Water Elemental, Air Elemental

#### Regenerates

Heals itself over time, so slow damage barely scratches it. Fire is the usual answer: most regenerators can't heal while Burning.

Units: Troll

#### Glass cannon

Big damage, little HP. Protect it and it wins fights. Let anything reach it and it's gone.

Units: Grunts

#### Raider

Built to reach what's behind the front line: archers, casters, siege and Keeps. A raider picks those out when they're close, so it doesn't cross the field for them: it gets there by speed, by flying, or by being placed on a flank. Guard your flanks, and keep something tough near your back line.

Units: Wolf Riders, Giant Bats

### Elements

#### Resistance

Every hit has an element. A unit's card lists its **resistances** (it takes less of that element) and **weaknesses** (it takes more). Armor is physical resistance: arrows bounce off Knights, but fire goes straight through.

#### Toughness

A few great beasts are so tough that every hit they take is a flat amount lower, whatever its element. Small hits, like a goblin's arrow, barely scratch them, while a ballista bolt hardly notices the difference. Even the smallest hit always does at least 25% of its damage. Burning and other damage over time aren't hits, so toughness doesn't stop them.

#### Physical

Swords, arrows, claws and boulders. Most attacks are physical, and armor (physical resistance) is common, so tough units shrug a lot of it off. A physical hit shatters a Frozen target.

- On Frozen: **Shatter** ×2 damage, removes Frozen

Attacks with it: Knights, Crossbowmen, Ballista, Griffon Riders, Dwarven Pikemen, Grunts, Skirmishers, Wolf Riders, Troll, Ogre Brute, Giant Bats, Cloudlings, Stone Golem, Giant, Bog Toad Riders

#### Fire

Fire goes straight through armor, and many big creatures take extra fire damage. It lights oil and stops most regeneration while a unit is Burning. Fire doesn't melt ice or dry anything off: it just burns, until water puts it out.

- On Oiled: **Ignite** +60 fire damage, becomes Burning

Attacks with it: Fire Elemental, Dragon

#### Water

Water soaks its targets. Wet on its own does nothing, but it sets up lightning and frost. Water also puts out fires.

- On Burning: **Douse** removes Burning

Attacks with it: Water Elemental

#### Frost

Frost chills its targets, slowing them down. Hit a Chilled or Wet target with frost and it freezes solid, ready to shatter.

- On Wet: **Freeze** becomes Frozen
- On Chilled: **Freeze** becomes Frozen

Attacks with it: Frost Elemental

#### Lightning

Lightning can jump from target to target. It hits Wet targets twice as hard, electrifies puddles, lights oil and shatters Frozen targets.

- On Wet: **Conduct** ×2 damage
- On Frozen: **Shatter** ×2 damage, removes Frozen
- On Oiled: **Ignite** +60 fire damage, becomes Burning

Attacks with it: Air Elemental

### Statuses

Statuses stick to a unit for a few seconds. Applying one counts as its element hitting the unit, so statuses trigger reactions. Like everything elemental, they land on both sides.

#### Wet

Soaked by water. It sets up lightning and frost. Lasts 5s.

- lightning → **Conduct** ×2 damage
- frost → **Freeze** becomes Frozen

#### Burning

On fire. A Burning unit also can't regenerate, unless it has learned to. Takes 14 fire damage a second, for 3s.

- water → **Douse** removes Burning

#### Chilled

Slowed by the cold. Another frost hit freezes it. Moves at 60% speed, attacks at 70% speed, for 3s.

- frost → **Freeze** becomes Frozen

#### Frozen

Frozen solid. The next physical or lightning hit shatters it for double damage. Can't move or attack, for 2s.

- physical → **Shatter** ×2 damage, removes Frozen
- lightning → **Shatter** ×2 damage, removes Frozen

#### Shocked

A flicker of stun from lightning. Can't move or attack, for 0.4s.

#### Oiled

Covered in oil. Fire or lightning ignites it for a burst of extra fire damage. Moves at 80% speed, for 5s.

- fire → **Ignite** +60 fire damage, becomes Burning
- lightning → **Ignite** +60 fire damage, becomes Burning

#### Dazed

Shaken. When a Keep falls its whole army is Dazed, and a few attacks daze whatever they hit. Deals only 10% damage, takes ×2 damage, for 4s.

#### Snared

Stuck fast, by a toad's tongue for one. A Snared flier is held so low that even swords and claws can reach it. Can't move, and a flier can be hit by attacks that only reach the ground, for 2.5s.

### Surfaces

Surfaces lie on the ground and affect ground units standing in them, on both sides. Fliers ignore them. Only some upgrades create surfaces, so the ground fills up only when someone chooses it.

#### Puddle

Standing water. It soaks anyone who stands in it. Lasts 12s. Every half second, anyone standing in it: applies Wet.

- lightning → Electrified Water (spreads through the connected area)
- frost → Ice

#### Electrified Water

A puddle struck by lightning. The charge spreads through the whole connected puddle. Lasts 2.5s, then turns back into puddle. Every half second, anyone standing in it: 18 lightning damage, applies Shocked.

- frost → Ice

#### Ice

Frozen water. It chills anyone on it, and melts back into a puddle when it runs out. Lasts 6s, then turns back into puddle. Every half second, anyone standing in it: applies Chilled.

#### Fire

Burning ground. It spreads through oil and burns whoever stands in it, on either side. Lasts 4s. Every half second, anyone standing in it: 10 fire damage, applies Burning.

- water → Steam

#### Steam

Water poured onto burning ground. Units inside can't be targeted from range, and the steam scalds them. Lasts 4s. Every half second, anyone standing in it: 6 fire damage. Units inside can't be targeted from range.

#### Oil

Slick oil. It slows units down, and fire or lightning turns all of it into burning ground. Lasts 15s. Every half second, anyone standing in it: applies Oiled, moves at 75% speed.

- fire → Fire (spreads through the connected area)
- lightning → Fire (spreads through the connected area)

#### Reactions

When an element hits a unit that already has a status, or ground that already has a surface, they react. Putting one surface onto another counts as its element hitting it: a puddle poured onto fire becomes steam.

| Element | hits a unit that is | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| lightning | Wet | **Conduct** ×2 damage |
| frost | Wet | **Freeze** becomes Frozen |
| frost | Chilled | **Freeze** becomes Frozen |
| physical | Frozen | **Shatter** ×2 damage, removes Frozen |
| lightning | Frozen | **Shatter** ×2 damage, removes Frozen |
| water | Burning | **Douse** removes Burning |
| fire | Oiled | **Ignite** +60 fire damage, becomes Burning |
| lightning | Oiled | **Ignite** +60 fire damage, becomes Burning |

| Element | hits the ground | Becomes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| lightning | Puddle | Electrified Water (spreads) |
| frost | Puddle | Ice |
| frost | Electrified Water | Ice |
| water | Fire | Steam |
| fire | Oil | Fire (spreads) |
| lightning | Oil | Fire (spreads) |
