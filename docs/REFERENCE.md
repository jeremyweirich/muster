The framework

The community describes the core loop as three roles in a rock-paper-scissors cycle: chaff, chaff clear, and carry. 
finalboss

Chaff beats carries by soaking up their big hits.
Chaff clear beats chaff.
Carries beat chaff clear and everything bigger.

On top of that cycle sits a target-size axis: light, medium, giant, and titan. Most “counters” really come down to damage profile versus target size:

Splash damage and many small hits are efficient against light units.
Big single hits are efficient against medium units.
Damage that ramps up over time, or deals a percentage of HP, is efficient against giants.

Supply cost tiers run 100, 200, 300, 400, 500, and 800.

What this means for your roster

1. Start around 16 units. Mechabellum’s early-access roster was 16: Vulcan, Fortress, Marksman, Melting Point, Rhino, Wasp, Mustang, Steel Ball, Fang, Crawler, Overlord, Stormcaller, Sledgehammer, Hacker, Arclight, and Phoenix. That roster split into:

3 chaff
4 chaff clear
4 anti-giant
1 anti-medium
2 tanks
1 utility
1 late-game giant

That ratio is a proven minimum viable roster, and a good template for your first pass.

2. Chaff clear should be your biggest bucket. Chaff is the cheapest answer to every big-damage unit. To keep chaff from dominating, you need answers to it at every range, every cost tier, and for air. Mechabellum kept only 3 real chaff units but grew chaff clear to 10.

3. Sledgehammer is the game’s punching bag, and that is useful to you. At least 8 of the later units list it as a unit they beat. The developers seem to treat it as the reference medium unit and tune new units against it. Since your knight is your anchor, copy that pattern deliberately. The knight should lose to:

a cheap ranged anti-medium unit (Void Eye or Vortex style)
a splash unit that punishes clumped mediums (Scorpion style)
a diver that ignores its frontline role (Sandworm style)
every giant

It should beat light chaff-clear units (Hound style) and other short-range mediums.

4. Ground versus air is a separate axis. 7 of the 33 units are air. Every role needs at least one answer that can shoot up, or air becomes an uncounterable win condition.
