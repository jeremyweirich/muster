# Playtest: the independents, 2 November 2026

The owner's goal for this round: work out what the independents are for. They exist to patch holes in each faction's
board at a higher cost, there was room for one more, and the missing role wasn't clear. Also balance the existing two
so they fill their role. The method is in `docs/PLAYTESTING.md`.

## Findings

1. **The missing role is anti-medium.** Splitting `coverage` by faction: the Wardens lack chaff and native splash, the
   Goblins lack anti-medium, long range and real anti-air, and the Elementals lack anti-medium and a diver. Anti-medium
   is the only role missing from two factions that no independent covered. The matches agree: the goblins couldn't kill
   medium casters behind a line, and had only chaff against medium fliers.
2. **The Goblins' worst hole is the air.** Every goblin unit lost 100% to the Dragon, and Skirmishers (chaff) were their
   only anti-air. Massed Barbed Skirmishers do work against Bats and Cloudlings.
3. **The old independents didn't patch anything in play.** The Giant is ground-only, and one squad costs 600 the first
   time, so the AI answered it for free by flying. The Dragon (800 the first time) is a lump the AI's ordinary anti-air
   kills in about 20 s. In one-unit fights it was the opposite: the Dragon won almost everything (Nash 16%, top mean
   margin), and the Giant beat the units meant to counter it (Frost 0%, Pikemen 0%, Wolf Riders 38%).
4. **The goblins' chaff clear and bruiser are both large**, so Ballistae (×2 against large) and Pikemen (×3.5) shut
   them down. Toad Riders with Spit Bile are a medium chaff clear, and they were the goblins' best units in g3.
5. Not about independents, for the next balance pass:
   - **Ballista parks behind chaff are untouchable.** In g3, up to 11 Ballistae took 0 damage for three rounds; Bats
     and Flanker Wolf Riders died on the Grunt wall in front. The October report saw the same with archers.
   - **The AI's Wolf Riders** (flanks, promoted early, with Clerics) decided two matches. They were already toned down
     on Nov 1; worth watching.

## What changed

**New independent: Bog Toad Riders** (owner-approved pitch). Swamp folk on giant toads who hire out to whoever fights
near their marsh. Their tongues are an instant beam drawn as a lick (`look.beam: "tongue"`), range 5, hitting ground and
air, ×1.8 against medium units. Three toads, 380 HP, speed 16, `class:cavalry` (so pikes punish them). 200 supply,
200 unlock.

| Upgrade | Effect | Intent (`src/data/balance.ts`) |
|---|---|---|
| Fat Toads | +40% damage | Deeper anti-medium |
| Spit Bile | splash 0.8, −40% damage | Mustang: a chaff clear that hits air (beats Cloudlings and Skirmishers) |
| Gulp | every lick Snares its target for 2 s | Hold melee at tongue's length (beats Knights), and pull fliers down for your own melee |
| Long Tongues | +2 range | Outreach the casters (beats the Fire Elemental) |

**New status: Snared** (the owner's net idea). Held in place, and a Snared flier can be hit by attacks that only reach
the ground. It's one exception in `canTarget`, driven by `grounded` on the status, so movement and collisions are
untouched. It has a Codex entry and green strands drawn over the token. With Trolls, it's the goblins' Dragon answer:
`fight "toad_riders,troll*2" dragon` goes from 0% to 100% with Gulp, keeping 92% of the army's value.

The first draft was Centaur Hunters with bows. The owner wanted fewer archers and something stranger, so it became the
toads. Monster Hunters (×2.5 against large) was replaced by Gulp.

**Giant:** 3200 → 2600 HP; boulders 215 every 3.2 s → 120 every 2.4 s. Smaller, faster boulders still flatten goblins
(100% against Grunts and Skirmishers), but no longer wipe the units meant to counter it: Wolf Riders 38% → 94%,
Trolls 75% → 100%, Frost Elementals 0% → 50–63% (a coin-flip). It now has 7 hard counters. **Pikemen
against the Giant was flipped in the table**: a packed line of light bodies is what splash artillery is for, as Fire
Elementals already show. The Wardens' Giant answers are Ballistae and anything that flies.

**Dragon:** 4200 → 3600 HP; breath 95 → 130. It used to time out against the Stone Golem (50%); now it wins (97%).

**Toad Riders' relations:** they beat Griffons, Bats and Air Elementals 100%. They lose to Pikemen, Grunts, Skirmishers,
Cloudlings (75%), Crossbowmen (the Wardens' own answer is better, as it should be), Stone Golem and Wolf Riders (they
get there first). Gulp doesn't flip Wolf Riders: twenty ×5 javelins land before the snares matter, as Rhino beats
shooters in Mechabellum.

## Numbers after (`npm run balance -- check`, 8 seeds)

- **95 of 97 relations hold.** The misses are coin-flips at 50%: Troll vs Ogre (known) and Frost vs Giant (63% at 16 seeds).
- **36 of 38 upgrade intents hold.** The misses are the known Ballista *Reinforced Frame* and Fire *Living Inferno*.
- Nash mix: Crossbowmen 52%, Cloudlings 16%, Dragon 14%, Skirmishers 9%, Grunts 5%, Wolf Riders 3%. Mean value margin:
  Dragon 63 (top), Toad Riders −1, Giant −9.
- `npm run bench`: 22.1 ms per battle (21.8 before). The fingerprint changed because the roster changed.

## Open

- **The Dragon is still too broad in one-unit fights**: it hard-counters 12 of 20 units (the cap is half), mostly
  ground units that can't hit air. In matches it dies fast, so this mostly says one-unit fights flatter fliers.
- **Self-TTK** is far above the bands for the Giant (52 s) and Dragon (63 s). Titans run long, as the Golem does; decide
  whether they get their own band.
- **No hard counter at 2:1** for the Toad Riders, as for Crossbowmen and Frost.
- **Ballista parks behind chaff** (finding 5).
- Not yet checked in a full match: Gulp + Trolls against a real Dragon, and Elementals using the Toad Riders.
