# Playtest and balance notes: the elemental redesign, 31 October 2026

Written after the owner's revision notes on the elementals, the Wardens and one goblin upgrade. It records what changed,
what the numbers and two full matches showed, and what's still open. The earlier file, `docs/PLAYTEST-2026-10.md`,
still holds the structural findings (veterancy snowball, splash vs swarms, AI timing); this one doesn't repeat them
unless they showed up again.

DESIGN.md stays the source of truth for the design. This file is evidence and advice. All numbers are level 1, no
upgrades unless stated, automatic placement. The E, D and G tables were measured just before Cloudlings lost their
lightning resistance (the last change); that moved Cloudlings’ E from −50 to −68, within noise.

that can learn to fly.

## 2. A bug in the yardstick

Experiment E ("add one squad vs the same supply of Elven Archers and Knights") buys filler in steps of 100. A 150-supply
unit was compared against **one** archer squad (100), and a 250 unit against 200. So every 150-cost unit got a free 50
supply, and every 250-cost unit a free 50 too. That's why Pikemen, Ballista, Wolf Riders and Cleric all sat at the top
of E in the earlier file.

**Fixed** in `scripts/balance-experiments.ts`: units whose cost isn't a multiple of 100 are added in pairs against twice
the filler, and the result is halved. Old roster, corrected (8 seeds): Pikemen +185 → +41, Wolf Riders +171 → −65,
Ballista +170 → +2, Cleric −25 → −144. **The per-unit E numbers in `PLAYTEST-2026-10.md` §5 are inflated for every
150- and 250-cost unit.** Treat them with that in mind.

The elemental reference army also changed, to include the Fire Elemental so its upgrades get measured: Water, Fire,
Air, Cloudlings, Frost (950). The Stone Golem left it.

## 4. Two matches as Elementals against the Hard AI (`npm run playtest`)

| # | My build | Result | What happened |
|---|---|---|---|
| A (seed 7) | Frost + Cloudlings, then Air (Eye of the Storm, later Take Flight), Golem and Water late | Lost 0–5 | The AI took the discounted Griffons and doubled them. Griffons dove my 350-HP shooters every round. I had no front line until round 4: my mistake as much as the roster's |
| B (seed 11) | Golem + Cloudlings, then Frost, Fire (Meteor), Overcast, Quake | Lost 1–3 with a draw, stopped at 93 HP vs 379 | R1: a 90 s draw. Cloudlings dealt 4,518 to Knights that can't hit air but couldn't finish them through armor. R2: the AI unlocked Frost Elementals, which killed 16 Cloudlings and took 0 damage. R3: **won**, with the Fire Elemental as MVP (5,724 damage, 24 kills, 147 friendly fire). R4–5: Warded Plate on promoted Knights (Lv3 by round 5) ground through |

What the matches showed:

1. **The enemy back line is untouchable.** The AI's Elven Archers took **0 damage in 7 of the 8 rounds they fought**,
   and its Frost Elementals in 3 of 4. Range 8 outranges every elemental except Meteor, and the elementals have no
   unit that reaches the back line: flying Air is range 4 and dies to the archers it dives at. Same pattern as §2.8 of
   the earlier file, but sharper for Elementals.
2. **The AI picks up the new units.** It answered Cloudlings with Frost Elementals in round 2, and elementals with
   Warded Plate in round 4. That's the punch/counterpunch working.
3. **The four-body Fire Elemental reads well**: a cluster of fireballs, with friendly fire small enough to ignore
   (150–230 a battle).
4. **Cloudlings are a real chaff flier**, but physical hail into armor is slow, so they time out against Knights. Freezing
   Rain (through armor) is the counterpunch, and it reads clearly.
5. **Veterancy still snowballs** (Lv3 Knights by round 5). Nothing here changes §2.1 of the earlier file.

## 5. Findings and suggestions

1. **A ground Air Elemental at speed 14 runs ahead and dies alone**, like the old Troll. Speed was the lever, more than
   HP, damage or range: speed 10 took it from about −80 to about 0 at 200 supply. Take Flight restores the speed. Rule 4
   of the earlier file ("a faction's front line is not its fastest unit") applies to anything on the ground that's fast
   and fragile.
2. **Air cost.** You asked for "slightly more expensive". It's 200 (unchanged), which is more expensive than the new
   Frost (150). At 250 it scored about −250 per squad, at either speed. Flip `cost` if you meant more than that.
3. **Cooling Mist (+1) and Cleric Purify (−22) do almost nothing on average.** Statuses on your own side rarely decide
   these fights. Both are pure counter-upgrades (against Conduct, Burning and the Daze when a Keep falls), and they
   overlap. Widening Cooling Mist to 6 tiles didn't help. A later pass could separate them, for example with Cooling
   Mist removing only Burning and Wet and Purify keeping the rest.
4. **Elementals get in each other's way.** Inside one army, frost snuffs Burning, fire thaws Frozen, and fire boils
   puddles into steam that blocks ranged attacks. So Downpour + Freezing Rain scores −77 next to Fire Elementals. Without
   them it's the best pair in the game: next to physical allies, 0/6 → 6/6 through Shatter. Tidal Surge (−112) has the
   same problem. This is the "elementals are dangerous to their friends" theme working as designed, but players will
   need the Codex to see why their combo fizzled. A good tutorial beat for the elemental campaign.
5. **Downpour + Thunderstorm is very strong.** Three Cloudling squads kill two Knight squads in 6 s instead of 38 s. It
   costs 600 supply, so it may be fine. There's also an order quirk: traits fire in purchase order, so if Thunderstorm is
   bought before Downpour, the opening strike lands before the soak. Consider sorting periodic traits so soakers go
   first.
6. **Goblins lose to every faction at level 1** (0% / 25% / 0%). Skirmishers are −171. Spacing (0.35), range 4.5 and
   faster shots each changed nothing. The problem is structural: 10-HP bodies at range 3.5 against range-8 archers and
   splash. Left alone this pass beyond the grenade change, because the goblin rework is recent and two slots are still
   placeholders.
7. **Physical damage is now 14 of 20 main attacks** (Cloudlings moved from lightning to hail). Lightning is down to the
   Air Elemental plus Thunderstorm. That's what you asked for, but the earlier file flagged physical as already
   over-represented.
8. **Elven Archers are the strongest supply in the game.** "+200 of archers" beats almost every upgrade in every faction
   army, and archers took no damage in play. If the elemental back line is meant to compete, the likeliest lever is the
   archers' range (8), not the elementals.

## 6. Open questions for the owner

- **A name for "special ability on its own timer".** Shriek, Thunderstorm and Improvised Bombs all use the same thing:
  a `periodic` trait with `atTarget` (and now `once`). Players never see a name for it; descriptions just say "every
  10s" or "once". If you want one, "ability" with a Codex entry would let descriptions link it.
- **Air Elemental price** (finding 2).
- **Does any elemental unit reach the back line?** Right now none does (finding 4.1). Options: a longer-range Frost
  upgrade, Air's Take Flight with more range, or accept it as the Elementals' weakness.
- **Goblin balance**: wait for the campaign, or do a pass now?
